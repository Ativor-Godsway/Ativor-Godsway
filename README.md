> **Shared rides across the University of Ghana, Legon. Flat GHS 5 fare.**
> Founder & Lead Developer · launched June 2026 · **2,000+ registered users**

Live map tracking, driver dispatch, and a flat fare that lets students heading the same way split one trip instead of each paying full price to go solo.
